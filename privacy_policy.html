1. Introduction

This privacy policy outlines how the "Google Ads Reporting Script" (the "App") handles data. This App is an internal tool designed to programmatically access Google Ads account data for reporting and analysis purposes. It is not intended for public use.

2. Data We Collect

When you authorize this App, we access the following data from the Google Ads API, in accordance with the permissions you grant:

  • Google Ads account information (Account names, IDs)
  • Campaign, Ad Group, and Ad performance data (Clicks, Impressions, Cost, Conversions, etc.)
  • Account structure and settings

This App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. How We Use Your Data

The data accessed is used exclusively for the following purpose:

  • To generate internal performance reports and conduct analysis of our own Google Ads accounts.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

We do not store your Google Ads data on any server. The data is processed in memory by the script during its execution and is used solely to generate a report. No data is saved permanently by the App.

We do not share, sell, or transfer your data to any third parties.
